About

I studied at Shanghai International Studies University from 2022 to 2026, majoring in Data Science and Big Data Technology.

I independently initiated research since my freshman year, and I have now completed two full first-author research projects, which were respectively published in EMNLP 2026 Findings and IALP 2024 (IEEE / EI).

Research Directions

My core research interests lie in NLP, bias, and safety. From a linguistic perspective, I am particularly drawn to complex pragmatics, discourse analysis, and Systemic Functional Linguistics. I hope to use these perspectives to make model behavior more interpretable, in dialogue with mathematical and computational methods, while engaging critically with AI ethics.

Current

After graduating from university, I have already developed new research ideas and directions that I would like to pursue further.

At this stage, I am actively seeking research collaborators, as well as support in computation, resources, and related expertise, to help advance these projects and turn them into impactful work. I am also open to future 2027 Fall PhD opportunities that align with these directions.

First Author · EMNLP 2026 Findings

Chinese Discriminatory Language Implicit Bias Context-Dependent Harm Anchor Preservation Contextual Priors ChLGBT Dataset Bias Calibration Interpretability
At the beginning of my junior year, I independently identified the research problem and developed the overall methodological framework. I proposed MAAM to address implicit bias and context dependence in Chinese discriminatory language, and constructed the ChLGBT dataset containing 8,120 manually annotated samples. I independently handled research design, theoretical derivation, dataset construction and annotation schema development, experimental code, analysis, and paper writing, and I also handled the ARR review response and iterative revisions during the revision process.
